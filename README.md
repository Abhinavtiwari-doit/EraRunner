🕹️ EraRunner
“No Internet? No Problem. Time to Evolve.”

Short Description:
EraRunner is an evolution-themed endless runner game inspired by Chrome Dino, where every level teaches you facts from prehistoric life to modern history. The more you run, the more your character evolves — turning internet downtime into a fun learning adventure.

💡 Inspiration

🦕 History is often taught in ways that feel boring or repetitive — and we wanted to change that. With EraRunner, our mission was to turn a simple offline moment into a time-traveling experience. Inspired by the Chrome Dino game, we asked ourselves: What if losing internet connection could trigger a journey through evolution?
When your connection drops, your time machine switches on!

⚙ What EraRunner Does

🦕 EraRunner transforms the classic offline runner concept into an educational adventure. As players clear levels, they unlock facts about different eras in Earth’s history — from early life to modern humans. Each level evolves your character, showing the beautiful progression of life through time.
The more you win, the more you learn.

🔧 How We Built It

🦕 EraRunner is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

-We started with a basic running mechanic and expanded it with:
-Multiple eras and levels
-Character evolution stages
-Fact cards that match each timeline
-Smooth transitions as the player moves through history

ach evolutionary phase is tied to a specific era, making the learning experience seamless.

💪 Challenges We Faced

🦕 Syncing character evolution with era-based facts was our toughest challenge.
We also struggled to design clear transitions between life forms — from early organisms to humans — while keeping the gameplay smooth and engaging.
Balancing fun and education required multiple iterations.

📌 Accomplishments We’re Proud Of

🦕 We created a game that players can enjoy as a quick break while still learning something new.
Blending fun mechanics with meaningful knowledge was our favorite achievement.
